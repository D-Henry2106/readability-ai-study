# SEO / Indexing Rules

SEO is intentionally limited because this is a research experiment, not a marketing site.

- `/` may be indexed.
- Sitemap contains only `/`.
- `/admin/**`, `/experiment/**`, `/consent`, `/demographics`, `/complete`, `/withdraw`, `/exit` and `/api/**` are disallowed/noindex.
- Experimental reading material, questions, answers and participant sessions are not emitted into sitemap metadata, Open Graph payloads or public searchable endpoints.
- No analytics/marketing tracking is installed.
